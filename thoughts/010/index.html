I read this post, and TBH, I really liked it. Right after reading it, I deleted my vibe-coded AI slop project from GitHub, even though I was actively working on it.

He’s right, actually. I believe that, before vibe coding, what allowed us to build great software was making mistakes—a lot of mistakes. Sometimes, a mistake could even turn into a feature. But now AI has taken away that kind of playing around, that kind of experimentation, that kind of ambition. So maybe we should be less ambitious when it comes to AI slop projects.
